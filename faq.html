🚀 Getting started
What is Tokproof and how does it work? ›
Tokproof lets you create trust pages — dedicated landing pages that sit between your TikTok bio link and your Shopify store. Instead of sending followers directly to a product page (which TikTok may downrank), you send them to a Tokproof page that builds confidence with reviews, social proof, and FAQs — then redirects them to buy. See an example →
What's the difference between a Trust Page and a Simple Page? ›
A Trust Page is optimised for a single product: it shows a video-style hero, social proof (reviews and TikTok comments), FAQs, and a single strong CTA that links to Shopify. Best for product launches and high-ticket items.

A Simple Page is a premium link-in-bio. It shows your avatar, bio, and a list of links to different products, WhatsApp, your store, or any other URL. Best for creators who promote multiple items.
Do I need technical knowledge to use Tokproof? ›
Not at all. Tokproof is built for creators and brand owners, not developers. You fill in a form, pick a template, and your page is live in minutes. No code, no hosting, no domain needed.
🎵 TikTok
How do I add my Tokproof link to my TikTok bio? ›
1. Publish your Tokproof page from the dashboard.
2. Copy your URL (e.g. tokproof.app/@skinglow) from the plan bar.
3. Go to TikTok → Edit profile → Website → Paste your URL.

You need a TikTok Business Account with 1,000+ followers to use the bio link feature.
Will TikTok allow my Tokproof link? ›
Yes — Tokproof pages are designed to comply with TikTok's link-in-bio policies. We use clean URLs, no auto-redirects, and a visible destination. That's exactly what the Safe Link Score measures: the higher your score, the safer your page is from TikTok restrictions.
Can I use Tokproof with TikTok Shop? ›
Yes. Tokproof works alongside TikTok Shop. You can link your CTA directly to a TikTok Shop product or to your Shopify store. Many creators use Tokproof as a trust layer that converts browsers into confident buyers before they hit the checkout.
📄 Pages
How many pages can I create? ›
On the Free plan, you can publish 1 page. You can create as many drafts as you want, but only 1 can be active at a time.

On the Pro plan, you get unlimited published pages — ideal for promoting multiple products or running seasonal campaigns. See Pro pricing →
Can I change my @username after creating my account? ›
Yes, you can change it in Settings → Account. Keep in mind that changing your username will update your public URL. Any old links shared externally will stop working, so it's best to avoid changing it once you've shared it widely.
What is the Safe Link Score and how do I improve it? ›
The Safe Link Score is Tokproof's trust rating for each of your pages, from 0 to 100. It checks factors like URL cleanliness, legal footer presence, CTA honesty, and meta tags. Visit the Safe Link Score page to see your score and personalised recommendations for improving it.
Can I use a custom domain? ›
Custom domains (e.g. link.skinglow.com) are available on the Pro plan. On Free, your URL is tokproof.app/@yourhandle. Upgrade to Pro →
📊 Analytics
What analytics does Tokproof track? ›
Tokproof tracks: total visitors, CTA clicks, click-through rate (CTR), traffic sources (TikTok, Direct, Instagram, etc.), device types, top countries, and a real-time activity feed. On Pro, you also get advanced breakdowns and CSV export.
How accurate is the CTR measurement? ›
CTR is calculated as CTA clicks ÷ total page views × 100. A click is counted each time a visitor taps your primary CTA button. The average Tokproof CTR is around 3.5%. Anything above 5% is excellent.
💳 Billing
How does the Pro plan billing work? ›
Pro is billed monthly ($19/mo) or annually ($15/mo, saving 2 months). You can cancel at any time — your Pro features stay active until the end of your current billing period. We accept all major credit and debit cards.
Is there a free trial for Pro? ›
The Free plan is permanently free and lets you explore Tokproof without a credit card. We're working on a Pro trial — sign up for our newsletter on the homepage to be notified when it launches.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription? ›
Yes, any time from Settings → Billing. There's no cancellation fee. After cancellation, your account reverts to the Free plan at the end of the period — your pages and data are not deleted.
